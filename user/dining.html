Our Kitchen Heritage

Cooked with Firewood, Clay Pots & Heartfelt Passion

At Serendiva, every meal is an authentic Sri Lankan ritual. We grind our own roasted curry powders daily from whole coriander, cumin, and true Ceylon cinnamon bark.

Slow earthen clay pot simmering allows spices to infuse deeply into fresh seafood brought daily from the southern coast, heirloom red rice harvested from local paddies, and wild herbs plucked from our garden.
