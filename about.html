About the Resort

Our Story & Background

A peaceful nature resort located in the hills of Ella, offering eco-friendly chalets and warm Sri Lankan hospitality.

Background

Built in Harmony with the Landscape

The name Serendiva comes from Serendib, an ancient historical name for Sri Lanka. The concept behind the resort was simple: provide travelers with a calm, comfortable place to stay amidst the misty tea hills and forest views of Ella.

We built each chalet using local timber and stone, keeping the natural curves of the hillside intact to safeguard mature trees and nearby water streams.

“Our goal is to provide restful, eco-conscious hospitality that highlights the natural beauty and culinary traditions of Sri Lanka.”

Key Highlights

Eco-Friendly Practices

Our open kitchen prepares traditional rice and curry dishes using clay pots and spices sourced from local village farms.

Community Employment
