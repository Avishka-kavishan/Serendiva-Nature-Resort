Ayubowan & Welcome

Peaceful Stays in the Hills of Ella

Serendiva Nature Resort is located along the mountain slopes of Ella, surrounded by tea plantations and natural forest. Built using local timber and stone, our chalets offer a quiet place to relax with fresh mountain air and scenic valley views.

During your stay, you can swim in our outdoor pool, taste home-cooked Sri Lankan curries, or join morning walking tours along nearby nature trails.
