Travel Access

How to Reach Us in Ella

✈️ From Colombo Airport (BIA)

Approximately 4.5 hours by highway. Private car hire can be scheduled ahead of your arrival.

🚂 The Scenic Hill Country Train

Regular trains connect Kandy and Nuwara Eliya with Ella Station. We offer station pickup.

🏖️ From the South Coast

Around 2.5 to 3 hours drive from Mirissa or Yala National Park through the Wellawaya mountain pass.
